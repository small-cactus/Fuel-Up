Fuel Up Privacy Policy

Fuel Up is provided by an independent developer. This policy explains the information used to show nearby fuel prices, provide recommendations, and operate optional Driving Research.

Using Fuel Up

No account is required. Fuel Up uses location, when permitted, to find nearby stations and provide relevant station information. Your fuel type, preferred brands, memberships, and other settings help personalize the app. Station information and reported fuel prices come from third-party sources. Station data, prices, and preferences may be cached on your device.

Requests to our servers and service providers may include the location needed for the requested feature and technical information needed to deliver or troubleshoot it. Fuel Up may process app interactions, device and app versions, crash reports, performance information, and other diagnostics to operate and improve the app. Information you send in a support request is used to address that request.

Optional Driving Research

Research is optional. Collection starts after you accept the research consent and grant the required device permissions. With background location permission, it can continue while the app is not on screen.

Research records may include:

  • Precise locations, timestamps, location accuracy, speed, course, and station geofence events.
  • Motion classifications such as driving, walking, running, cycling, or stationary, together with confidence information.
  • Step counts and walking distance for limited time windows around detected station visits, when Apple's Motion & Fitness services make these available.
  • Arrival and departure observations, station identifiers, detected visits, notification events, and your answers about whether you stopped or bought fuel.
  • Permission state, app and operating-system version, background-refresh and low-power state, collection errors, and other information needed to evaluate collection reliability.
  • A randomly assigned participant ID, session and event identifiers, upload counts, and a push-notification device token.

The participant ID is not your name, but the records are not anonymous: persistent identifiers and precise locations can link records to a person or device. A detected stop is an inference. An unanswered confirmation is not treated as a confirmed fuel purchase.

We use these records to research driving and station-stop detection, evaluate features, improve recommendations, and diagnose failures. Fuel Up does not collect HealthKit medical records or a continuous raw accelerometer stream for this research.

Research uploads and notifications

Research events are stored locally before syncing. Automatic bulk uploads normally wait for Wi-Fi and at least 500 pending samples, then drain the queued samples. Manual Sync Data uploads all pending samples using an available connection, including cellular.

While research is enabled, small control requests and periodic sample-count reports may use cellular data. An authorized research administrator can request a sync for a participant ID; when the app can process the request, queued records may upload over cellular as well as Wi-Fi. Device scheduling, connectivity, and your collection controls affect when uploads occur. These operations may use your data allowance.

With notification permission, Fuel Up can ask you to label station visits and notify you of collection problems. Apple Push Notification service is used for remote notifications. Research test notifications and their responses are marked separately in the stored research records.

Sharing and service providers

Fuel Up does not sell your personal information or use research records for advertising tracking across other companies' apps or websites. Research records are processed on our backend using Supabase. Apple provides maps, device location and motion services, and push delivery; station and routing services process information needed for the features they provide. Hosting, diagnostics, and other service providers may process information to operate the service.

Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect the service and its users. If ownership changes, information may transfer with the service, subject to applicable notice requirements.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded research events are retained on the research backend until you request deletion through the app. Older, successfully uploaded local events may be removed after 30 days; unsent records remain queued. Pausing collection does not delete existing records.

Use the delete control in Settings > Driving Research to remove research events from the active research database and your device. Deletion requires a network connection and two confirmations. If it cannot complete, collection is paused and local records are retained so you can retry. The backend revokes the participant credential and removes associated event records, sync requests, counts, notification-test records, and push registrations. A minimal revoked-participant record is retained to prevent further use of that credential.

Deleting the app removes its local app data but does not itself request deletion of previously uploaded research records. Use the in-app deletion control before uninstalling, or contact support with your participant ID. Copies in operational backups may remain until those backups expire.

Your choices

You can decline research, pause it, sync manually, or delete its records from the research screen. You can change Location, Precise Location, Motion & Fitness, and notification access in iOS Settings. Revoking permissions can limit the related features. Core station browsing does not require research enrollment.

Security and international processing

Fuel Up uses encrypted network connections and access controls to protect information. No system is completely secure. Service providers may process information in countries with different data-protection laws.

Children

Driving Research is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied research records, contact support so the records can be addressed.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The published policy will show its effective date. Material changes to research collection should be communicated before collecting additional data that requires consent.
