# Exploratory prospective refuel replay

Select the participant with the most server research events at export time. Use a fixed server-receipt cutoff, retain the export privately, and commit only code and aggregate results. This is a single-person exploratory replay, not a validated model or a change to the app.

Ground truth: last explicit participant answer per visit. `fueled` is a positive; `not_fueling` and `not_a_stop` are answered negatives. Unanswered, unsure, and wrong-station events remain unknown. Arrival means the first observed station-stop timestamp, which may be later than physical arrival. Evaluate at 2, 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes before that timestamp. Future visit records/answers define outcomes only, never prediction inputs. Historical answers are usable only after their recorded/label time. Sensor values are usable only after their device receipt/record time; server upload time separately measures server-side feasibility.

Predeclare simple hypotheses before running metrics:

- Habit: most frequently previously confirmed station; recency breaks ties. A station guess is not a prediction that fuel will be purchased.
- Fuel interval: predict a refuel in the next 15 minutes when time since the last known fueled visit is within 15 minutes of the median of previous positive intervals; requires three previously answered fuel visits (two intervals). No inferred purchases or tank levels.
- Approach: moving at least 2.5 m/s, within 2 km and more than 200 m from a previously known station, heading within 45 degrees toward it. Predict a visit within 15 minutes at the closest qualifying station. Evaluate both prior-confirmed stations and station IDs actually recorded in earlier catalog/geofence observations. Catalog geometry, if used, comes from current static station metadata and is an explicit replay limitation; no future station ID is admitted.

Use the latest valid fix available before each prediction, no more than 120 seconds old; reject simulated, invalid, stale, and accuracy above 40 m. Missing data means abstain. Also replay signals at most once per recorded moving minute; enforce a 20-minute alert cooldown per policy. Describe alert burden and distinguish matched confirmed fuel, explicit non-fuel, unconfirmed station encounters, and alerts with no known labeled outcome. Never turn an unanswered stop or an unobserved interval into a negative. No threshold search or selected best score; report every predeclared horizon and baseline. Separate first-ever fuel cold start, later fuel opportunities, and exact station correctness.

Exclude arrival/dwell, departure, pedometer, system-visit callbacks, prompts, future labels, future route segments, current prices, retrospective tank estimates, and detector mode from prediction features. Mode may already encode nearness to a station. Motion flags and driving history can be described only as of the cutoff; a saved event does not prove the app was backgrounded throughout the trip. Counterfactual predictions do not establish ability to wake iOS or deliver a notification.

## Secondary exploratory route hypothesis

After examining the baseline results, add a separately reported route-template hypothesis without threshold tuning: compare the current moving fix to recorded route points from 1–30 minutes before already answered visits. Require at most 300 m separation and heading within 45 degrees. The nearest eligible point determines the earlier visit label, station, and historical time-to-stop; a prior explicit negative suppresses the prediction. Both the historical fix and its visit answer must be available before the query. Predict within 30 minutes. This additional exploration is not an independent held-out confirmation and is not selected as a replacement for the baseline results. Report continuous alerts with the same 20-minute cooldown, and matched-arrival timing errors. Also check distance to the static station center and time relative to first recorded entry within 250 m; these are approximate geometry checks, not pump arrival truth.
