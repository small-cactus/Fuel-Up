## Fixes

- Persist onboarding grade and radius atomically; serialize preference writes and refresh permissions on foreground return.
- Remove the twice-per-second camera timer, GPS extrapolation for display, and delayed unanimated refit. MapKit fits with measured card and safe-area padding when actual location/station targets change. Ignore GPS jitter.
- Remove the selected chip's border. Suppressed annotations unmount as a unit instead of fading native glass through zero alpha (documented glass limitation).
- Reserve a compact, balanced reset row above the native tab bar with a 44-point hit target.
- Native SwiftUI fuel picker, including E85; radius preview subscribes to actual permitted device location. No fabricated location when permission is absent.
- E85 provider, validation, cache and trend support. Never relabel another grade's price/validation as E85; equal diesel/E85 and regular prices remain independently valid.
- Extract Home location bootstrap, map geometry, camera target policy, probe planning/telemetry and carousel card into focused modules. Further controller extraction is still in progress.
